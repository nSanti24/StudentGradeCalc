## Student Grade Calculator (1st Year, 1st Sem, Finals Project)

Welcome to BSIT 1A Group 4's "Student Grade Calculator"! Here is everything you need to know about our project:

## Project Description

Homework/Assignments (30%), Quizzes (30%), and Final Examination (40%). It features an interactive menu that allows users to calculate grades, view the grading rubric and percentage breakdown, or exit the application. The program uses reusable methods, do-while loops, and switch-case statements to organize its functions and manage menu navigation.

To ensure accurate calculations and reliable input handling, the application uses for loops to collect multiple homework and quiz scores, while validation loops and try-catch statements handle invalid inputs and prevent errors. It also applies if-else statements to determine the corresponding letter grade based on the final weighted score. Once all scores are processed, the program displays a structured, receipt-style summary showing the category averages, final weighted grade, and equivalent letter grade.

## Features Added in Our Project Include:
- `Main Menu` – *Provides users with options to calculate their weighted final grade, view the grading rubric and weight breakdown,or exit the program.*
- `Weighted Final Grade Calculator` – *Calculates the student's final grade based on the weighted percentages of homework, quizzes, and the final exam.*
- `Grading Weight System` – *Applies the assigned grading percentages: Homework (30%), Quizzes (30%), and Final Exam (40%).*
- `Final Weighted Grade Calculation` – *Combines the homework average, quiz average, and final exam score according to their respective weights.*
- `Rubric and Weight Breakdown Display` – *Shows the grading components and their corresponding percentages.*
- `Final Grade Receipt` – *Displays a summary containing the homework average, quiz average, final exam score, final weighted grade, and corresponding letter grade.*
- `Formatted Grade Display` – *Presents calculated grades with two decimal places for a cleaner and more readable output.*

and many more!

## Guide for Student Grade Calculator
1. Enter student information:
    - `Section Name`: Enter the name of your section.
    - `Subject Code`: Enter the subject you are calculating your grades with.
    - `Number of Students (0-100)`: - Enter the number of students you are calculating grades for.
2. Choose from 1-3 in the **Main Menu** options:
  **Main Menu Options**
    - `Option 1 (Insert Grades / Calculate)`: Records the prelim, midterms, and finals grade you input before calculating.
    - `Option 2 (View Rubric & Weight Breakdown)`: View the grades rubric and grades weight breakdown.
    - `Option 3 (Exit)`: Exits the program.
    
  **Choose option 1 to insert your grades for each student and start calculating:**
    - `Enter Student Name`: Enter the names of the students.
    - `Enter Prelim, Midterms, and Finals Grade`: Enter the grade of your prelim, midterms, and finals grade.

3. Once you have entered all students' grades, **Grade Options** menu will display. Choose between options 1-3:
     **Grade Options:**
     - `Option 1 (Show Calculated Grades)`: Shows a table of the calculated grades.
     - `Option 2 (Insert Grades Again)`: Records grades again, keeps the student information until you go back to the main menu or exit the program.
     - `Option 3 (Back to Main Menu)`: Takes you back to the main menu.
